Auto-send messages

After Maya reads your message back and you say yes, a bubble shows "Sending to Jen in 3s · Tap to cancel", then she taps Send for you in WhatsApp, Messenger and Messages. Works for group chats on WhatsApp and Messenger too. Without it, the chat opens ready and you tap Send.

Off

Turn on: tap Turn on → Installed apps → Maya auto-send → switch it on. If it's greyed out, tap Allow restricted settings below first (⋮ menu, top right of Maya's app info), then try again.

Routines

Things Maya does by herself at a set time and says out loud. Just tell her: "every weekday at 6, give me my morning brief", "every Friday at 8 PM remind me to pay the bills" or "tomorrow at 5, tell me when to leave for the station".

Morning brief

Weather, today's calendar, your to-do list, missed messages and birthdays, in a few sentences. Needs the Claude or Open brain.

Message reading

Lets Maya see your message notifications (WhatsApp, Messenger, texts, email). Ask "who messaged me?" or "what did I miss?", and reply by voice. She always reads your reply back and waits for your yes.

Off

Turn on: tap Turn on → Maya message reader → Allow. If it's greyed out, first tap Allow restricted settings in the Auto-send section above.

Driving mode

While it's on, Maya reads new messages aloud, and you can reply or call by voice with the phone still locked. It switches on by itself when the phone connects to your car's Bluetooth. Needs Message reading, and Maya left open in the background.
